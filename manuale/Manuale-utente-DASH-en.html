Part 1 · Why DASH

1. The problem

People who work for clients and on projects today do not lack tools: they have too many. Requests arrive by email, in meetings and in chats; commitments sit in the calendar, in To Do and often only in memory. None of these tools tells you the most important thing: what is slipping through the cracks.

  • A request you had to answer sinks to the bottom of the inbox, under later emails.
  • An approval stays stuck for days and you notice when the project is already late.
  • You wrote to someone, they did not reply and nothing reminds you.
  • After a client meeting, the promised follow-up never starts.
  • You walk into a meeting without remembering what is pending with those people.
  • A “can you send it by Thursday?” written in a chat ends up on no list.

To keep everything under control you have to reread email and chats every day, or fill in yet another list by hand. Often the problem only comes to light when the reminder arrives.

2. What DASH solves

DASH is a small panel, on your computer and on your phone, that helps you keep track of your everyday work. It runs on Windows, Mac, iPhone and iPad and Android. It reads your mail, your calendar, your To Do tasks and, if you wish, your Teams chats, and shows you at a glance:

  • Overdue: missed deadlines, requests you have not answered by the date asked, meetings with external people with no follow-up;
  • Stalled approvals: approvals requested from you or by you that have been waiting for too many days;
  • Waiting on me: people waiting for your reply;
  • I’m waiting on: your requests that have not yet had a reply from the person you wrote to (or their company): if only a colleague in copy replies, the signal stays and says so. If you wrote to colleagues, a reply from any colleague is enough.

You do not have to enter anything: DASH works on the data you already have in Microsoft 365. It does not replace Outlook, Teams or your project tools: it works alongside them.

Part 2 · What DASH does: the features

3. The DASH screen

The pictures come from a Windows PC; on the Mac and on the phone the screen is the same. This part applies to all devices: where something differs it is stated here, and the rest is in the chapter for your device (10 to 13). On the phone, where you read “click”, tap.

At the top: the icon, the DASH name with its version and, if set, your company logo. On the right, three buttons:

  • ↻ refreshes now (DASH refreshes on its own every 5 minutes anyway);
  • ⚙ opens Settings;
  • – puts DASH back on the desktop, behind the other windows (computer only).

Below: greeting, next meeting, clock and weather.

Then the Radar (chapter 4) and, further down, Agenda (today’s and the next days’ meetings, with «Join» for Teams meetings), Calendar (week or month) and To Do (tick to complete, «▸ note» to read the task’s note, a field to add a new one).

Each section opens and closes when you click its title; DASH remembers your choices.

4. The Radar

The Radar has four blocks, closed at start-up: the number next to the title tells you how many signals there are (red for «Overdue» and «Stalled approvals»). Click the title to open them.

Each signal shows who, the subject, for how many working days it has been waiting (Saturdays, Sundays, New Year's Day and Christmas Day do not count, nor do the holidays you add in Your public holidays) and a line of explanation. A red border marks what is urgent or past due. Without AI, requests, approvals and deadlines are recognised in Italian, English, German and French, whatever the language of the app.

Click a signal to open it: the full subject and the text appear (the first lines of the email or Teams message, or the note of the To Do task). Click again to close it; to read everything use Open.

Tags: Teams (purple) if the signal comes from a Teams chat; AI if it was read by artificial intelligence.

Actions on a signal

On the computer, move the mouse over a signal to see the actions; on the phone they are always visible below the signal:

ActionWhat it does
Reply / OpenOpens the email or meeting in the Outlook app (or the chat in the Teams app). If the app is not there, it opens the web version.
ReadOpens the whole signal in a larger window: who, subject, the summary and all the new text of the email or message. “Back to the Radar” closes it.
Move to…Puts the signal in another group of the “By contact” view (see below). The choice also applies to future signals.
Follow upDrafts a polite follow-up that you can edit. «Copy and open in Outlook» copies it and opens the email: paste it into your reply. With AI on there is also «Rewrite with AI». The follow-up text is written in Italian.
ResolvedIt is done (perhaps in person or by phone): the signal disappears.
IgnoreThe signal is not relevant: it disappears.
Ignore similarCreates a rule: from now on, emails with the same sender or recipient and a similar subject do not enter the Radar (e.g. a client’s weekly reports). If the subject is too short to recognise similar emails, the rule would apply to all emails from that person: DASH first asks for a second click to confirm.
CompletedTo Do tasks only: marks the task as completed.

Click a signal to open it: you see the text and the actions. The reminder action prepares a text you can edit and then copy into your reply.

The read action shows the whole signal, with the summary and all the new text of the email or message.

Signals hidden with Resolved or Ignore become visible again if something new arrives in the same conversation. In Radar rules › «Review hidden signals» you can find them and bring one back with «Show again»; «Show them again» brings them all back.

Morning summary

At 8:30 on working days DASH sends a notification, which on Windows stays on screen until you close it. At the top of the Radar, every day from the first update, you find the “Today’s Radar” box: the morning’s pending items, split by block (“Overdue” and “Stalled approvals” open, the others with one click). During the day the box updates itself: what you close stays crossed out with ✓, what arrives later appears with the “new” label, and at the top you see how much you have done (“3 of 9 done”). Click an item to open it; click the title to reduce the box to one line and to open it again. For now the summary text is in Italian. You can turn the summary off in Radar rules.

The morning summary box: what is pending this morning, divided by block, with how many you have already done.

The Radar by contact

At the top of the Radar choose “By type” (the four blocks) or “By contact”. In the second view DASH groups the signals on its own: by company for people writing from outside (from the email domain: alfa-assicurazioni.it becomes “Alfa Assicurazioni”), by person for colleagues and personal mailboxes. Each group says what is pending, for example “Alfa Assicurazioni · 2 waiting for you · 1 overdue”.

The view by contact: signals grouped by company or person, with what is pending.

Nothing to set up. If a group is wrong, open the signal and use Move to…: type the group name (also a new one, for example “Administration”) and choose whether it applies only to that person or to their whole company. Automatic group goes back to DASH’s suggestion. Your choices sync with your other devices.

Outlook flags and categories

An email you flag in Outlook becomes a to-do in the Radar: under “Waiting on me”, or under “Overdue” once the flag’s due date has passed. A flagged sent email goes under “I’m waiting on” as a follow-up. When you mark the flag as complete in Outlook, the signal closes on its own. The “DASH: importante” category brings an email into the Radar even if a rule would exclude it; “DASH: ignora” does the opposite.

With Flag emails from DASH turned on (chapter 5), signals show Flag and Complete: the flag is set and completed in Outlook too.

Ready for the meeting

In the Agenda, next to meetings with other people, there is Prepare. It opens a card you can read in a minute: what is pending with the participants (also with their colleagues at the same company) and the latest exchanges of the last two weeks, each one opens with a click. With artificial intelligence on, 3 summary lines also appear at the top: the AI only receives subjects and details, never the text of emails.

The meeting preparation card: what is pending with the participants, then the latest exchanges.

15 minutes before (Windows and Mac only) the meeting a notification arrives: click it to open the card. You can turn the reminder off in Radar rules.

5. Radar rules

Open them with Rules, at the top right of the Radar.

  • «I’m waiting on» after: how many working days without a reply before one of your requests is flagged (3 by default).
  • Stalled approvals after: the same for approvals (2).
  • Meeting follow-up within: days after a meeting with external people without an email from you to the attendees (2). It does not apply to meetings you declined or to meetings organised by others with more than 8 external people.
  • Your public holidays: the holidays not to count, separated by commas. 15/08 applies every year, 24/12/2026 only once. In the DASH calendar these days, New Year's Day and Christmas Day are shown in red.
  • Summary at 8:30: turns the morning notification on or off.
  • Reminder 15 minutes before meetings: turns the “Ready for the meeting” notification on or off.
  • Also read Microsoft Teams: see chapter 6.
  • Outlook categories to ignore: emails with these categories do not enter the Radar (by default «DASH: ignora»).
  • Important Outlook categories: emails with these categories always appear in the Radar, even if a rule would exclude them (by default «DASH: importante»).
  • Group by Outlook category: in the “By contact” view, emails with a category (e.g. “Alfa project”) go into that category’s group.
  • Flag emails from DASH: Flag and Complete buttons on signals. Requires the permission to modify mail (chapter 8) and a new sign-in.

Emails to leave out

Each rule has two fields; if you fill in both, both must match:

  • From/to: an exact address (name@client.com) or a domain (client.com, subdomains included);
  • Subject: one or more words separated by commas; one is enough.

Example: From/to client.com and Subject report, weekly export leaves out that client’s reports, but not their other emails.

An Outlook rule works too: DASH only reads Inbox and Sent Items, so emails moved automatically to another folder (e.g. «Reports») are already outside the Radar. Replies to your requests at least one day old (up to 40 at a time), however, are recognised even if they ended up in another folder.

At the bottom of the panel, Excluded in the last update (click to open) lists the emails left out and the reason: useful to check that your rules do what you want. Left out because they do not look like requests lists the emails addressed to you that DASH did not treat as requests, starting with those containing a deadline or an approval. Remember to click Save.

6. Microsoft Teams

With Also read Microsoft Teams on (Radar rules), DASH also considers:

  • 1:1 chats: if the last message is from the other person and asks you something → «Waiting on me»; if it is yours, with a question unanswered for some days → «I’m waiting on»;
  • group and meeting chats: only messages that mention you (@) or start with your name;
  • team channels: messages where you are mentioned, until you write in the thread.

The first time, DASH may ask you to sign in again: Microsoft must confirm the Teams permissions. If Teams cannot be read, a red line «Teams cannot be read» with the reason appears under the Radar title.

7. Settings and sync

  • Language of DASH: Automatic (that of Windows, the Mac or the phone), or Italiano, English, Deutsch, Français. Buttons, signal texts and dates change at once; User manual also opens this document in the chosen language. Texts written by the AI, follow-ups and notifications stay in Italian.
  • Application (client) ID and Tenant: your organisation’s, entered at the first start; change them only if your administrator asks you to.
  • Keyboard shortcut to bring up DASH (computer only; by default Control+Alt+D).
  • Weather location: «Automatic» or a city.
  • Start DASH with Windows, on the Mac the option to open DASH when you log in to the Mac (computer only).
  • Open emails and meetings in the Outlook app: if off, the web version always opens.
  • Sync with your other devices (computer and phone): see below.
  • Company logo in the header (computer only): «Choose logo…» (PNG, JPG or SVG up to 1 MB), «Remove logo».
  • At the bottom: Radar rules…, Artificial intelligence…, User manual (computer only, opens this document), Sign out.

Syncing computer and phone

If you use DASH on several devices (for example your PC and your iPhone), turn on Sync with your other devices on each of them. From then on they share:

  • the Radar rules (days, excluded categories, rules, «Ignore similar», Teams, summary) and the artificial intelligence choice;
  • signals marked Resolved or Ignore, and «Show them again»;
  • the emails to leave out;
  • the AI analyses: an email read by Copilot on one device is not read again on the other.

What stays on each device: Client ID and Tenant, the language, the Anthropic and OpenAI keys, the keyboard shortcut, starting with Windows, the logo and the AI costs. Sync happens at every update and a few seconds after each of your actions; the time of the last sync appears under the checkbox. Rules on emails not to consider that are added on different devices are combined, and those removed stay removed; for the other values in the Rules the last change wins.

The data is kept in a file in a folder reserved for DASH in your OneDrive (Apps › DASH): do not move or edit it. The first time, DASH may ask you to sign in again, because Microsoft must confirm the permission for that folder.

8. Connecting your mail

DASH reads data from your organisation’s mail server. Today the supported platform is Microsoft 365 (Outlook, calendar, To Do and Teams); when other platforms become available, their instructions will be in this chapter.

Microsoft 365 and Teams

DASH uses an app registration in Entra ID with delegated permissions: each person sees only their own mailbox, and data travels only between users’ devices and your organisation’s Microsoft 365 (TMAC receives nothing). Each organisation uses its own tenant and, for artificial intelligence, its own Copilot licence or Anthropic or OpenAI key.

Once for the whole organisation, the administrator registers DASH in your tenant as follows and gives users the Client ID and Tenant (or the domain name), to be entered at the first start.

App registration (5 minutes)

  1. https://entra.microsoft.com › Identity › Applications › App registrations › New registration.
  2. Name: DASH. Account type: Accounts in this organizational directory only.
  3. Redirect URI: platform Public client/native (mobile & desktop), value http://localhost. Register.
  4. In Authentication: «Allow public client flows» = Yes. No client secret: it is not needed.
  5. In Overview copy the Application (client) ID and the Directory (tenant) ID: these are the values to enter in DASH.
  6. In API permissions add the permissions in the table below and click Grant admin consent.

9. Setting up artificial intelligence

If you want to use AI, choose the engine on each device, or on one device with sync turned on (chapter 7). The permissions for Copilot are in chapter 8.

It is optional: without AI the Radar works with rules and costs nothing. With AI a model reads the text of candidate emails and messages and recognises implicit requests, polite reminders and commitments made in words; it also writes the follow-ups.

Open it from Settings › Artificial intelligence… Choose the Engine:

  • Rules only: no cost, no data sent;
  • Microsoft 365 Copilot: no extra cost if you have a Microsoft 365 Copilot licence; data stays in Microsoft 365. After saving, DASH may ask you to sign in again. Click Test Copilot to check;
  • Anthropic API (Claude): requires a key from the Anthropic Console, pay as you go. Paste the key, Save and Test the key. Set a spending limit in the Console.
  • OpenAI API (ChatGPT): requires a key from the OpenAI platform, pay as you go; a ChatGPT subscription is not enough. Paste the key, Save and Test the key. Set a spending limit on the platform.

Maximum emails read per day is a safety limit (150 by default); each email is read only once. With Anthropic and OpenAI you must also tick the authorisation to send the data, on each device: without it no data is sent and the Radar works with its rules. You withdraw it by unticking the box or choosing “Rules only”. The box below shows how many emails were read today and, for Anthropic and OpenAI, the estimated cost for the month. The texts written by the AI (summaries, follow-ups) are in Italian for now.

Part 4 · Installation: one section per platform

Read only the chapter for your platform, then the first sign-in.

10. DASH on Windows

You need a PC with Windows 10 or 11 and the file DASH-Setup-1.6.6.exe.

Installation

  1. Double-click DASH-Setup-1.6.6.exe.
  2. If Windows shows «Windows protected your PC» (SmartScreen), click More info and then Run anyway. The warning appears because the program is not yet digitally signed: only go ahead if you received the file from a source you trust.
  3. Choose whether to install just for you (recommended) and the folder, then Install. No administrator rights are needed.
  4. When it finishes, DASH starts on its own. You will find its icon on the desktop and in the Start menu.
DASH starts automatically when you turn on your PC. You can change this in Settings › «Start DASH with Windows».

Showing and hiding DASH

DASH behaves like a widget: it sits on the desktop and, when you click another window, it moves behind the other apps. It has not closed: to bring it to the front use the shortcut Ctrl + Alt + D, or click the DASH icon in the notification area (next to the Windows clock), or show the desktop (Win + D).

Right-clicking the icon in the notification area opens a menu with Aggiorna ora (refresh now), Impostazioni (settings), Avvia con Windows, Ripristina posizione (reset position), Esci dall'account (sign out) and Chiudi DASH (close DASH). For now this menu is in Italian only.

Updating and uninstalling

  • Updating: close DASH (right-click the icon › Chiudi DASH) and install the new version over the previous one. Settings, rules and sign-in are kept.
  • Uninstalling: Windows Settings › Apps › Installed apps › DASH › Uninstall. The settings remain in the %APPDATA%\DASH folder: delete it if you want to remove everything.
Installed? Now go to chapter 14, “First sign-in”.

11. DASH on the Mac

On the Mac DASH is the same program as on Windows. You need a Mac with an Apple processor (M1 or later): this version does not open on Macs with an Intel processor.

Installation

  1. Double-click the file DASH-1.6.6-mac.dmg.
  2. In the window that appears, drag the DASH icon onto the Applications folder. Then close the window and eject the «DASH» disk (right-click › Eject).
  3. Open Applications and double-click DASH.
  4. If macOS says it cannot open it because the developer cannot be verified: go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down to «DASH was blocked…», click Open Anyway and confirm with your Mac password. This only happens the first time.
  5. At the top right, in the menu bar, the DASH D appears and the panel opens. If it asks to send notifications, choose Allow: they are used for the 8:30 summary.
  6. Enter the Application (client) ID and Tenant, then Save and Sign in (chapter 14).
  7. If you see «DASH wants to use your confidential information stored in your keychain», type your Mac password and choose Always Allow: that is where DASH keeps your session, encrypted.

What is specific to the Mac

  • The DASH icon is in the menu bar at the top right, not in the Dock. Clicking the icon shows or hides the panel; right-clicking (or a two-finger click) opens the menu, with Chiudi DASH (close DASH).
  • The keyboard shortcut to bring it up is ⌃ ⌥ D (Control + Option + D). You can change it in Settings, for example to Command+Shift+D.
  • Back to desktop hides DASH, which comes back with the shortcut or by clicking the icon in the menu bar.
  • Open takes you to Outlook on the web: classic Windows Outlook does not exist on the Mac.
  • Instead of «Start DASH with Windows» there is Open DASH when you log in to the Mac, in Settings.
  • The morning summary arrives as a Mac notification.
To have the same rules, hidden signals and AI analyses on the Mac as on your other devices, turn on Sync with your other devices (chapter 7).

Updating and uninstalling

  • Updating: close DASH (right-click the icon in the menu bar › Chiudi DASH), open the new .dmg file and drag DASH onto Applications, choosing Replace. Settings, rules and sign-in are kept.
  • Uninstalling: close DASH and move DASH from Applications to the Trash. The settings remain in ~/Library/Application Support/DASH: delete that folder if you want to remove everything.
Installed? Now go to chapter 14, “First sign-in”.

What is specific to the phone

The interface, the Radar, the rules and the artificial intelligence are the same as on the computer. This is what changes:

  • Compact header: time, date and temperature are on one line. Tap the line to open or close the forecast for the next days; DASH remembers your choice.
  • Actions always visible under each signal (Open, Resolved, Ignore, Ignore similar), because there is no mouse hover on a phone.
  • Updates: every 5 minutes while the app is open, and straight away when you return to it. With the app closed iOS does not allow reading mail: if you open it after many hours, the AI takes a couple of minutes to read the new emails.
  • Morning summary: arrives as a notification at 8:30 on working days, with the data from the last time you opened DASH. When you open the app you also find, every day, the “Today’s Radar” box at the top of the Radar, as on the PC. Allow notifications at the first start.
  • Open takes you to the iPhone’s Outlook or Teams app, if installed, otherwise to Safari.
  • Large screen: on an open iPhone Duo, on iPad or with the phone in landscape, DASH splits into two columns, with the Radar on the left and Agenda, Calendar and To Do on the right. When closed or in portrait it goes back to one column by itself.
  • There is no keyboard shortcut, no starting with Windows, no classic Outlook choice and no company logo.
  • The manual does not open from the app: you will find it at dash.tmac.it/assistance.html.

If you also use DASH on another device and want the same rules, hidden signals and AI analyses, turn on Sync with your other devices on both (chapter 7).

Uninstalling

First sign out and, if you entered an AI key, use “Remove the key”. Then touch and hold the DASH icon on the Home Screen › Remove App › Delete App.

Installed? Now go to chapter 14, “First sign-in”.

13. DASH on Android

Installation

What is different from iPhone

  • The session and the Anthropic and OpenAI keys are encrypted in Android’s protected storage instead of the iOS keychain.
  • The Client ID and Tenant are the same; in Entra ID the administrator adds the Android platform (chapter 8).

Uninstalling

First sign out and, if you entered an AI key, use “Remove the key”. Then touch and hold the DASH icon › Uninstall.

Installed? Now go to chapter 14, “First sign-in”.

14. First sign-in

The first sign-in is the same on all devices. On the phone, where you read “click”, tap.

What you need, on any device

  • A Microsoft 365 account from your organisation (Outlook mail and calendar).
  • Your organisation’s DASH Client ID and Tenant: your Microsoft 365 administrator gives them to you. If this has not been done yet, chapter 8 is all they need.
  • Optional, for artificial intelligence: a Microsoft 365 Copilot licence or an Anthropic API or OpenAI API key (chapter 9).
  1. The first time, DASH opens Settings. Enter the Application (client) ID and the Tenant given to you by your organisation’s Microsoft 365 administrator (the Tenant can be the domain, e.g. company.com, or the directory ID). Click Save.
  2. DASH shows Connect Microsoft 365. Click Sign in.
  3. Your browser opens the Microsoft sign-in page: sign in with your work account.
  4. If Microsoft asks you to accept the permissions for «DASH» (read mail, calendar and tasks), accept. If you see «Admin approval required», contact whoever manages Microsoft 365 in your organisation (chapter 8).
  5. Go back to DASH: after a few seconds the greeting, agenda and Radar appear.
DASH uses your organisation’s Microsoft 365: your data stays between your device and your tenant. You can change Client ID and Tenant at any time in Settings (chapter 7).
Try DASH without Microsoft 365: type demo in the Application (client) ID field (no Tenant needed), click Save and then Sign in. DASH shows sample data, in the app’s language, with the “Demo mode” bar always visible; it doesn’t connect to Microsoft and doesn’t touch your data or rules. To leave: Leave demo, or clear the field or enter your Client ID.

15. Data privacy

  • DASH runs on your PC or your Mac and on your phone (iPhone or Android): there is no TMAC server in between. It reads only your mailbox, with your permissions. Nobody else, not even TMAC, sees your signals.
  • TMAC does not receive or see your data. The artificial intelligence DASH uses is your organisation’s: its Microsoft 365 Copilot licence or its Anthropic or OpenAI key, under its contracts. TMAC only provides the program.
  • With sync on, settings, hidden signals and signals extracted by the AI are also copied to the DASH folder in your OneDrive: they stay in your organisation’s Microsoft 365, never with TMAC.
  • On the device DASH keeps the settings, the hidden signals and, with AI, only the extracted signals (not the email texts). The Microsoft session and the Anthropic and OpenAI keys are encrypted with the system’s protection: Windows, the Mac or iPhone keychain, Android’s protected storage.
  • With AI on, the model receives only date, subject, names, domain and new text of the emails and messages the Radar evaluates: never full addresses, attachments, emails excluded by rules or automatic ones.
  • Copilot: data stays within your organisation’s Microsoft 365 boundary, under its contractual commitments (including not training models on customer data).
  • Anthropic API: under the commercial agreement, data is by default not used to train models; processing takes place outside the EU. Use it only if your organisation has approved it.
  • OpenAI API: by default data is not used to train models; OpenAI keeps abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days. Use it only if your organisation has approved it.
Legal guarantees (data processing agreement, transfers outside the EU, privacy notice) depend on the contracts with Microsoft, Anthropic and OpenAI and on the checks of your organisation’s privacy officer: this manual describes how DASH works and does not replace those checks.
